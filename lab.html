How the estimates work

Time = episodes × seconds. Action samples = time × Hz. Video frames = action samples × cameras. Video GiB = time × cameras × Mbps × 1,000,000 ÷ 8 ÷ 1,073,741,824. Constant bitrate assumed; excludes sensors, audio, metadata, checkpoints, retries and overhead.
