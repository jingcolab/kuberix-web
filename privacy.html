Know what this workspace does.

Your inputs stay local.

The planner operates in this browser. Draft saving is off by default; if enabled, inputs are saved in this website’s local storage on this device. Storage is not encrypted, backed up or synced. Turning saving off deletes the stored draft while leaving the form in memory. Clearing the workspace removes both. Clearing browser data can erase drafts.

No user accounts, tracking scripts, model APIs, cloud database or dataset upload are included. Hosting providers may process standard request metadata. Do not enter credentials, personal data, proprietary datasets or confidential robot configurations. Exported files are your responsibility.

A plan is not a training run.

Kuberix creates a planning JSON document. It does not execute Python, train or evaluate policies, connect to GPUs, inspect data, run MuJoCo or control robots. Exports are not LeRobot/MuJoCo configurations and are not directly executable. External tools require their own installation, infrastructure and version-specific setup.

Estimates are assumptions, not measurements.

Episode, sample and video storage estimates use manually entered values. Video size assumes constant encoding bitrate and excludes audio, robot state, metadata, checkpoints, retries and overhead. Nominal splits do not assign files or prevent data leakage automatically. Group related recordings before allocation. Checklist answers are self-reported, not validated.

Simulation first. Human supervision always.

Nothing in this website certifies robot safety or authorizes hardware operation. Use independently validated simulation, qualified human review, an appropriate risk assessment and hardware-specific safeguards before considering any physical deployment. Do not use generated plans as commands for safety-critical systems.

Images and external resources.

The robotic-arm hero is an AI-generated editorial image, not a live feed, product photograph or proof of hardware ownership. LeRobot and MuJoCo are independent third-party projects. Kuberix is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. External links open their own sites and privacy terms apply.
